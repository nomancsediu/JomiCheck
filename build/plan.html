1. Executive Summary

Land disputes are among the most common civil cases in Bangladesh. Most of them start before the sale: a seller who is not the recorded owner, land sold twice, an heir selling more than their share, land under litigation, mortgage, acquisition or khas status. An ordinary buyer cannot read and cross-check khatians, deeds, mutation records and legal status on their own.

JomiCheck performs, in minutes, the title search that an experienced land lawyer would carry out before a purchase. A citizen enters the plot identifiers (District, Upazila, Mouza, Dag, Khatian) and the seller's name, or uploads documents. JomiCheck then:

  1. Builds the ownership chain (বায়া দলিল) and checks it for gaps.
  2. Verifies that the seller has the legal right to sell: recorded ownership, inheritance share (ফারায়েজ), power of attorney validity, minor/deceased owner.
  3. Checks the status of the land: litigation, mortgage, khas, vested (অর্পিত), waqf, acquisition, tax arrears.
  4. Detects fraud: double-selling, over-selling, conflicting registration vs mutation, prior sale agreements (বায়নানামা), suspicious rapid transfers.
  5. Warns about pre-emption risk (হক-শুফা) from co-sharers.
  6. Produces a transparent risk score (Low / Medium / High) where every flag cites the relevant law and is explained in simple Bangla or English.
  7. Generates a tamper-proof report (QR + hash) with a document checklist and next steps.
One-line pitch: JomiCheck does an experienced land lawyer's pre-purchase title search in minutes — ownership, inheritance share, pre-emption, litigation, khas and acquisition — with the relevant law cited for every risk.

3. The Problem in the Bangladesh Context

Land information in Bangladesh is spread across multiple records, offices and survey generations:

RecordWhat it holdsOffice
Khatian / Porcha (খতিয়ান / পর্চা)Record of rights — owners and their sharesLand Records & Survey / AC Land
Survey generationsCS, SA, RS, BS (and City Survey) khatians — often conflictingDLRS
Dag (দাগ)Plot number within a MouzaMouza map
Deed (দলিল)Registered sale, gift (heba), exchange, partitionSub-Registry Office
Mutation (নামজারি), DCRUpdating the record of rights after transferAC Land Office
Land Development Tax (দাখিলা)Tax payment proofUnion Land Office
Legal statusCourt cases, acquisition, khas, vested property, mortgageCourts, DC office, banks

Common risks a buyer faces

RiskWhat happens
Ownership mismatchThe seller is not the person recorded in the latest khatian.
Double-sellingThe same plot or share is sold to more than one buyer.
Over-sellingAn heir or co-sharer sells more land than their legal share.
Registration vs mutation gapDeed registered but mutation never done, or done in another name.
Survey conflictsOwner or area differs between CS, SA, RS and BS records.
Restricted landKhas, vested, waqf, debottor, acquisition, forest, river-eroded land.
EncumbranceLand mortgaged to a bank or under an earlier sale agreement.
Pre-emption (হক-শুফা)A co-sharer later claims the land through court after the sale.
Power of attorney abuseSale through a revoked or unregistered POA, or after the principal's death.

The result is lost savings, years of litigation and family conflict. Citizens need a simple, trustworthy way to screen a plot before paying any money.

4. Solution Overview & Positioning

4.1 How it works

StepSystem action
1Citizen enters District → Upazila → Mouza → Dag → Khatian and seller's name, or uploads documents.
2System collects ownership, deeds, mutation, survey history, legal status, mortgage and tax records.
3OCR / extraction converts uploaded documents into structured fields.
4Verification engine cross-checks records (ownership, chain of title, survey lineage, mutation).
5Legal engine checks right to sell (inheritance share, POA, minor/deceased owner, Section 52A).
6Fraud engine detects double-selling, over-selling, prior bainanama, suspicious transfers.
7Risk engine computes a tiered, explainable score with legal citations.
8AI explains findings in Bangla/English; report is generated with QR + hash, checklist and next steps.

8. Risk Engine & Scoring Model

The engine uses deterministic, explainable rules first. Each rule produces a flag with: severity, points, evidence, legal reference, plain-language explanation and recommended action.

8.1 Severity tiers

TierExamplesEffect
CRITICALSeller is not the owner; double-selling; khas / vested land; acquisition in progress; revoked POAResult is HIGH regardless of total score
MAJORPending litigation; mortgage; over-selling share; mutation missing or mismatched; survey conflict; prior bainanama+20 to +30 each
MINORTax arrears; pre-emption exposure; chain-of-title gap; price below mouza rate; document field inconsistency+5 to +10 each

8.2 Rule weights (initial)

RuleTierPoints
Seller ≠ recorded ownerCritical+40
Same Dag / share in multiple active salesCritical+35
Khas / vested / waqf / debottor landCritical+40
Acquisition notified or in progressCritical+40
Sale through revoked / invalid POACritical+35
Pending litigation / injunctionMajor+30
Sale area exceeds legal share (over-selling)Major+25
Mortgage / lienMajor+25
Mutation missing / in another nameMajor+20
Prior registered bainanamaMajor+20
Survey generation conflictMajor+20
Conflicting transaction informationMinor+10

8.3 Score bands

Total is capped at 100. LOW 0–30   MEDIUM 31–60   HIGH 61–100   — any critical flag forces HIGH.

}

9. Demo Scenarios

Three prepared cases show the full range of the system. All data is synthetic.

Case 1 — Clean property  LOW 6/100

Dag 101, Khatian 210. Seller is the recorded owner, mutation is in his name, complete chain of title, no case or mortgage. Only a small tax arrear.

Recommendation: Low risk. Collect the listed documents, pay the tax arrear and proceed with a lawyer.

Case 2 — Inheritance over-selling  MEDIUM 55/100

Dag 118, 1.00 acre. Recorded owner (deceased) left a wife, two sons and one daughter. Seller Karim (a son) is selling 0.40 acre.

HeirFaraiz shareArea
Wife1/80.125 acre
Son (Karim)2/5 of remaining 7/8 = 7/200.350 acre
Son7/200.350 acre
Daughter7/400.175 acre

Flags: over-selling 0.05 acre (+25), mutation not done in heirs' names (+20), Hak-e-Shufa exposure from co-heirs (+10).

Recommendation: Buy only up to 0.35 acre, after mutation, with written consent of the other heirs.

Case 3 — Fraud  HIGH 91/100

Seller Rahim, Dag 125, Khatian 456. The latest record shows a different owner, and the same Dag appears in another active transaction.

Flags: ownership mismatch (critical, +40), double-selling (critical, +35), conflicting transaction information (+10), tax arrears (+6).

Recommendation: Do not proceed without official verification at the AC Land and Sub-Registry offices.

13.3 Rules for the dataset

  • Fictional names only (e.g. রহিম উদ্দিন, করিম মিয়া, ফাতেমা বেগম); fake NIDs like 0000000001.

  • Generator script committed to the repo; seed fixed so data is reproducible.
  • README statement: "All data is synthetic and generated by our script. No real personal data or government portal data is used."

QuestionAnswer direction

Is the risk score legally valid?No — it is decision support; every flag cites the law and recommends official verification.
Why not blockchain?We use hashing + QR for report integrity; a ledger can be added when official records are integrated.

Business model?Free for citizens; paid API for banks, lawyers and real-estate firms; government partnership.

Sustainability

  • Free basic check for citizens
  • Paid API / detailed reports for banks (collateral verification), law firms, developers and real-estate agencies
  • Government partnership as part of Smart Land Management / Smart Bangladesh initiatives

Expected impact

  • Fewer citizens lose money to fraudulent or risky land purchases
  • Fewer land-related civil cases over time
  • Better awareness of land law among ordinary buyers
  • Pressure on sellers to keep mutation, tax and records up to date

21. Limitations, Ethics & Disclaimer

  • JomiCheck is a decision-support and risk-screening tool, not official legal verification and not legal advice.
  • The prototype uses only synthetic data; it does not access any government system.
  • Results depend on the completeness and accuracy of the available records.
  • Inheritance calculation covers common cases; complex cases require a lawyer.
  • Personal data in a production system must be protected with consent, access control, encryption and audit logs.
  • Every report recommends confirming findings at the relevant government office and with a lawyer.

Summary: JomiCheck turns a lawyer's pre-purchase title search into a fast, transparent and citizen-friendly tool. It fits P5 directly, uses P3 fraud detection as its intelligence layer and P2-style hashing for report integrity, and it is designed to scale countrywide through authorized government integration.
